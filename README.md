🛒 E-COMMERCE DATA ANALYSIS | POWER BI

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce data using Microsoft Power BI to explore sales performance, product trends, inventory levels, ratings, and product risk indicators.

With the guidance and support of the Cozmek Pvt. Ltd. team, I worked on data cleaning, data modeling, and data analysis to transform raw data into an interactive dashboard for business reporting and data-driven decision-making.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze sales performance across product categories.
- Compare product prices and sales values.
- Examine monthly sales trends.
- Analyze product ratings and stock quantities.
- Monitor maximum and minimum product prices.
- Explore product risk indicators and inventory patterns.
- Present key metrics through an interactive dashboard.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI – Dashboard development and visualization
- Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
- Data Modeling – Structuring data for analysis
- Data Analysis – Exploring trends and performance indicators

📊 Dashboard Features

- Sales Analysis: Category-wise sales comparison and monthly sales trends.
- Product Analysis: Product prices, sales values, ratings, and price indicators.
- Inventory Analysis: Stock quantity comparison across products.
- Rating Analysis: Distribution of products by rating status.
- Risk Analysis: Classification of products into safe and risk categories.
- KPI Cards: Product count, stock quantity, maximum and minimum prices, and other displayed metrics.
- Interactive Filters: Year selection and date-range filtering.

🔍 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard visualizations:

- Category Performance: Bedsheets recorded the highest displayed sales value among the five categories, at approximately 42.6K.
- Product Comparison: The dashboard compares product prices and sales values for products such as Biography, Denim Jacket, and Mountain Bike.
- Monthly Trends: Sales values fluctuate throughout the displayed year, with a noticeable peak around July.
- Inventory Distribution: Biography shows the highest displayed stock quantity among the products listed in the stock analysis chart.
- Rating Distribution: Products are classified into medium, low, and high-rating categories.
- Product Risk: Approximately 76.7% of displayed products are classified as safe, while 23.3% are classified as risk products.

Note: These observations are based on the displayed dashboard visuals and may vary depending on the selected filters and date range.
